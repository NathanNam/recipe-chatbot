DIETARY RESTRICTION DEFINITIONS:
- Vegan: No animal products (meat, dairy, eggs, honey, etc.)
- Vegetarian: No meat or fish, but dairy and eggs are allowed
- Gluten-free: No wheat, barley, rye, or other gluten-containing grains
- Dairy-free: No milk, cheese, butter, yogurt, or other dairy products
- Keto: Very low carb (typically <20g net carbs), high fat, moderate protein
- Paleo: No grains, legumes, dairy, refined sugar, or processed foods
- Pescatarian: No meat except fish and seafood
- Kosher: Follows Jewish dietary laws (no pork, shellfish, mixing meat/dairy)
- Halal: Follows Islamic dietary laws (no pork, alcohol, proper slaughter)
- Nut-free: No tree nuts or peanuts
- Low-carb: Significantly reduced carbohydrates (typically <50g per day)
- Sugar-free: No added sugars or high-sugar ingredients
- Raw vegan: Vegan foods not heated above 118°F (48°C)
- Whole30: No grains, dairy, legumes, sugar, alcohol, or processed foods
- Diabetic-friendly: Low glycemic index, controlled carbohydrates
- Low-sodium: Reduced sodium content for heart health

Query: I want paleo bread but every recipe I've tried is terrible
Recipe Response: Absolutely! Let me share with you a delicious and foolproof Paleo Almond Flour Bread. It’s soft, flavorful, and perfect for toast, sandwiches, or simply slicing with butter. This recipe makes about 8 slices, serving 2-4 people.

### Ingredients:
- 2 cups almond flour
- 1/4 cup ground flaxseed
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 4 large eggs
- 1/4 cup coconut milk (full-fat or unsweetened)
- 1 tablespoon honey or maple syrup (optional, for a hint of sweetness)
- 1 tablespoon apple cider vinegar
- 2 tablespoons olive oil or melted coconut oil

### Instructions:

1. **Preheat the Oven:**
   Set your oven to 350°F (175°C). Line a standard 8x4-inch bread loaf pan with parchment paper, leaving some overhang for easy removal.

2. **Mix Dry Ingredients:**
   In a medium bowl, whisk together the almond flour, ground flaxseed, baking soda, and salt until well combined.

3. **Combine Wet Ingredients:**
   In a separate large bowl, beat the eggs until frothy. Add the coconut milk, honey (if using), apple cider vinegar, and olive oil. Whisk until smooth.

4. **Incorporate Dry into Wet:**
   Gradually add the dry mixture into the wet ingredients, stirring well after each addition, until you get a thick batter. It should be slightly sticky but spreadable.

5. **Transfer to Pan:**
   Pour the batter into the prepared loaf pan. Use a spatula to smooth the top evenly.

6. **Bake:**
   Place in the oven and bake for about 30-35 minutes, or until the top is golden brown and a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.

7. **Cool & Slice:**
   Let the bread cool in the pan for about 10 minutes. Then, transfer it to a wire rack and cool completely before slicing.

8. **Serve:**
   Enjoy slices plain, toasted with paleo-friendly toppings like avocado, or use for paleo sandwiches!

This almond flour bread has a moist, slightly nutty flavor, and the texture is tender yet holds together well. Perfect for your paleo diet!

Example 2:
Query: I'm vegan but I really want to make something with honey - is there a good substitute? i am craving a yogurt breakfast
Recipe Response: Absolutely! Since you're vegan and craving a yogurt-based breakfast with honey, I recommend making a delicious Vegan Coconut Mango Parfait. Instead of honey, we'll use agave syrup or maple syrup as a natural sweetener. This parfait is creamy, fruity, and perfect for a satisfying breakfast for two.

### Vegan Coconut Mango Parfait (serves 2)

#### Ingredients:
- 1 cup canned coconut milk (preferably full-fat for creaminess)
- 2 tablespoons agave syrup or maple syrup
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1 cup unsweetened coconut yogurt or any plant-based yogurt
- 1 ripe mango, diced
- 1/2 cup granola (choose a nutty or fruity variety)
- Fresh mint leaves for garnish (optional)

#### Instructions:

1. **Prepare the Coconut Cream:**  
   - Shake the canned coconut milk well before opening.
   - In a mixing bowl, combine 1/2 cup of the coconut milk with the agave syrup and vanilla extract. Whisk until well combined.
   - Chill this mixture in the fridge for about 15-20 minutes to help it thicken slightly.

2. **Create the Coconut Yogurt Layer:**  
   - In a small bowl, mix the coconut yogurt with the remaining coconut milk mixture to thin it slightly. This creates a creamy, flavorful yogurt base.

3. **Layer the Parfaits:**  
   - Take two glasses or jars.
   - Start with a spoonful of the coconut yogurt mixture at the bottom.
   - Add a layer of diced mango.
   - Sprinkle a bit of granola.
   - Repeat the layers until the glasses are filled, finishing with a layer of mango and granola on top.

4. **Garnish & Serve:**  
   - Top each parfait with fresh mint leaves if desired.
   - Serve immediately for a crunchy texture or refrigerate for 10-15 minutes if you prefer it chilled.

This parfait combines the tropical flavor of mango and coconut with the natural sweetness of agave or maple syrup, making it a delightful vegan alternative to a yogurt breakfast with honey. Enjoy!

Query: I avoid processed foods but I'm lazy
Recipe Response: Great! I have a simple and wholesome recipe that requires minimal effort: **Garlic Lemon Herb Shrimp**. It's quick, healthy, and perfect for lazy days. Plus, it uses fresh ingredients without processed foods.

### Garlic Lemon Herb Shrimp (Serves 2)

#### Ingredients:
- 200 grams (about 7 oz) raw shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice
- Zest of ½ lemon
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano or thyme
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
- Fresh parsley or cilantro, chopped (optional, for garnish)
- Lemon wedges, for serving

#### Instructions:

1. **Prepare the Shrimp:**
   Rinse the shrimp under cold water and pat dry with paper towels. Place them in a bowl.

2. **Make the Marinade:**
   In a small bowl, combine the minced garlic, olive oil, lemon juice, lemon zest, dried oregano (or thyme), salt, and black pepper. Mix well.

3. **Marinate the Shrimp:**
   Pour the marinade over the shrimp, tossing gently to coat all the pieces evenly. Let it sit for 10-15 minutes while you prep other ingredients or set your table.

4. **Cook the Shrimp:**
   Heat a large skillet over medium heat. Once hot, add the marinated shrimp in a single layer. Cook for about 2-3 minutes per side until they turn pink and are opaque. Be careful not to overcook.

5. **Serve:**
   Remove from heat. Garnish with chopped parsley or cilantro if desired. Serve immediately with lemon wedges on the side.

#### Serving Ideas:
Pair this with steamed vegetables, a simple salad, or whole-grain rice. It’s light, flavorful, and requires minimal prep!

Query: Sugar-free dessert that doesn't taste like sadness
Recipe Response: Absolutely! I recommend trying a **Baked Apple Cinnamon Dessert**—a warm, comforting treat that is naturally sweetened with the apples' own juices and a touch of cinnamon. It's sugar-free, full of flavor, and perfect for satisfying your sweet tooth without feeling like "sadness." Here's how to prepare it:

### Baked Apple Cinnamon Dessert (serves 2)

#### Ingredients:
- 2 large crisp apples (such as Fuji, Honeycrisp, or Gala)
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1/4 teaspoon ground nutmeg (optional)
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 2 tablespoons chopped nuts (walnuts or pecans) – optional
- 2 teaspoons unsalted butter or coconut oil
- A handful of fresh berries (like blueberries or raspberries) for garnish – optional

#### Instructions:

1. **Preheat your oven** to 375°F (190°C). This ensures it's hot enough to bake the apples evenly.

2. **Prepare the apples:** Using a peeling or coring tool, carefully remove the core from each apple, creating a cavity in the center. If you prefer, you can slice the apples into rings or halves, but cored whole apples look more like a classic baked dessert.

3. **Season the apples:** Mix the cinnamon, nutmeg (if using), and vanilla extract in a small bowl. Rub this mixture all over the apples, ensuring they’re well coated. If you like, fill the cavity with chopped nuts for added texture.

4. **Add butter:** Place a small teaspoon of butter or coconut oil into the cavity or on top of each apple to enhance richness and moisture.

5. **Arrange in a baking dish:** Place the prepared apples upright in a baking dish, cut side up. Pour a small amount of water (about 1/4 cup) into the dish to help keep the apples moist.

6. **Bake:** Cover the dish loosely with foil and bake for about 30-35 minutes, or until the apples are tender when pierced with a fork. The aroma of cinnamon and baked apple will fill your kitchen!

7. **Serve:** Carefully transfer the baked apples to plates. Optionally, garnish with fresh berries or a dollop of unsweetened Greek yogurt for added creaminess.

This dessert is warm, naturally sweet from the apples, and rich in cozy spices—definitely not sad! It's delightful on its own or with a scoop of sugar-free ice cream or Greek yogurt. Enjoy!

Apples are naturally high in sugar and are the primary ingredient, and the optional fresh berries also contribute significant natural sugars. The description explicitly relies on the apples' natural sweetness. Under the provided definition ('No added sugars or high-sugar ingredients'), this violates sugar-free. Optional unsweetened Greek yogurt also contains natural sugars (lactose), though the main issue is the apples.
